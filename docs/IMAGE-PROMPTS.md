# Image prompts for gokulkp.com (for ChatGPT image generation)

Goal: banner and section images that look like real photographs taken by a working photographer, not "AI art". Copy one prompt at a time into ChatGPT, generate, pick the best, and send me the file. I'll convert it to WebP, make the responsive sizes and place it.

## Two rules first

1. **No AI image may pretend to be proof.** Don't generate client work, ad results, dashboards with numbers, your face, or "you on set". Visitors and clients would be misled, and it undermines the real results on the site. The on-set photos on the About page, your portrait, and campaign screenshots must stay real.
2. **AI images here are mood and context only:** desks, gear, light, places, textures. Think of them as stock photos you directed yourself.

## Paste this at the start of every prompt (style block)

```
Documentary-style photograph, shot on a full-frame camera with a 35mm lens at f/2.8, ISO 800, available light only. Natural imperfections: slight film grain, mild lens vignetting, a few dust specks, uneven surfaces, real-world clutter that hasn't been tidied for the photo, imperfect focus falloff. Muted, slightly desaturated colour; deep near-black shadows (#0A0A0A) with one warm orange accent (#F97316) coming from a practical light or object in the scene, not a filter. Off-centre composition with negative space on one side for text. No text, no logos, no brand names, no watermarks, no readable screens, no faces. Must not look like CGI, a 3D render, an illustration or "AI art".
```

## Paste this at the end of every prompt (avoid list)

```
Avoid: glossy plastic skin or surfaces, perfect symmetry, glowing edges, floating particles, bokeh orbs, teal-and-orange movie grading, HDR look, over-sharpening, extra fingers or warped hands, melted or impossible objects, gibberish text, lens flares, fantasy lighting, "cinematic", "epic", "8K", "hyper-detailed".
```

If a result still looks too polished, reply in the same chat: *"Make it look like an unretouched photo from a phone or a mid-range camera: flatter light, more grain, slightly crooked framing, less contrast."*
